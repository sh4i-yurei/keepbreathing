Namaste! I'm Mark, and this is my corner of the internet. It started with a DOS prompt and a kid who wanted to know why his commands weren't working, then with a phone line and a modem that suddenly put the rest of the world within reach. I used it to talk to people I'd never have met otherwise and to go looking for things that weren't meant to be easy for a kid to find. I've been fixing computers for other people since my teens, and I have never once been able to walk away from something before I understand how it works underneath. That never wore off. After years spent making a living other ways, I'm finally going after it head-on. I'm 46, autistic, a father of four and stepfather of two, and this is where I write it all down as it happens.

I came to tech the long way around. I spent years in professional kitchens, did photography, and most recently delivery and courier work, and computers were the constant underneath all of it. Now I'm doing PC repair part time and studying Computer Networking and Cybersecurity at Hinds, building toward security work and eventually the machine learning and AI that's pulled at me for as long as I can remember. Going back to school wasn't a small decision and I didn't make it for a paycheck. It's me aiming everything I've got at the thing I kept circling back to.

What I love is the hands-on part, taking something apart to see how it actually works. For me that's networking and security labs, home lab and self-hosting builds, electronics and hardware, and learning how things break so I understand how to defend them. It runs through the rest of my life too. I keep a garden and cook out of it, I aquascape and keep fish, I fly fish, and I shoot photos, and I lean on Buddhism to keep a still mind through all of it. It's one kind of curiosity pointed in a lot of directions.

This is the journey, written down as I go. Stick around and see where it heads.
